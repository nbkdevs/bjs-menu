## Description (≤ 260 chars)

Pick a BJ's main, sides, drinks and desserts and see calories, protein, carbs, fat and sodium for the whole meal. Items with no published value are flagged, not guessed, and allergens are listed. Works offline; also a CLI and libraries.

## First comment (maker)

Hi PH! Restaurant nutrition PDFs make it hard to add up a whole meal, and most calculators quietly skip items without data, so totals look lower than they are. BJ's Meal Builder totals a meal from 183 menu items and marks any total that is missing a value with "+", naming the items. You can filter for lighter mains, more protein, less sodium or no milk/wheat. The full menu with prices lives on our BJ's menu site (https://bjsmenus.com/). Unofficial, not affiliated with BJ's Restaurants. Feedback welcome!
